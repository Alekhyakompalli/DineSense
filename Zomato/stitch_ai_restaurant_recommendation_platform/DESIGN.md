## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an atmospheric, hyper-premium dining intelligence experience. The aesthetic fuses culinary anticipation with cutting-edge algorithmic precision: visceral food passion rendered through a sleek, nocturnal lens.

### Brand Personality & Demographics
- **Tone:** Discerning, predictive, sensual, and authoritative.
- **Target Audience:** Epicureans, urban explorers, high-intent diners, and culinary travelers seeking bespoke restaurant discovery without cognitive overload.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires trust through contextual reasoning, excites the palate with vivid imagery against deep canvases, and creates effortless confidence in decision-making.

### Visual Architecture & Aesthetic Movement
The design movement is **Midnight Glassmorphism with Cyber-Luxe Accents**:
- **Canvas Base:** Obsidian and midnight charcoal backgrounds that make culinary photography and metadata radiate.
- **Atmospheric Glow:** Subtle violet-to-cyan radial mesh glows anchored in deep space, suggesting live algorithmic compute behind the surface.
- **Glass & Surface Depth:** Frosted glass panels layered with hair-thin luminous boundaries (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` to `0.12)` that float effortlessly over ambient lighting.
- **Vibrant Signposting:** Heritage crimson for primary dining conversion actions, radiant amber gold for prestige ratings and recommendations, and an electric violet-to-cyan gradient reserved strictly for synthetic intelligence reasoning and dynamic sensory breakdowns.

## Colors

The palette operates in strict hierarchies against a high-contrast dark spatial environment. Light is treated as an emissive element rather than a flat surface paint.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#E23744` / `#FF385C` - Electric Crimson):** The signature food-tech anchor. Reserved for primary calls-to-action (Book Table, Order Signature Dish), interactive highlights, active tab indicators, and critical conversion points.
- **Secondary (`#8B5CF6` to `#06B6D4` - AI Neon Violet & Cyan):** Reserved for generative mechanics: taste-match confidence scores, semantic reasoning cards, dynamic taste radars, and natural language prompts.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Warm Amber Gold):** The hallmark of culinary excellence. Applied exclusively to Michelin accolades, critic scores, star ratings, chef's selections, and premium loyalty perks.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#0B0F17` & `#111827`):** Obsidian void base (`#0B0F17`) acting as the absolute ground plane, with Midnight Charcoal (`#111827`) providing architectural scaffolding and foundational panels.

### Surface Color Layers
- **Surface Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#0B0F17`
- **Surface Level 1 (Structural Shell / Nav / Drawer):** `#111827`
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Cards / Modals):** `rgba(26, 34, 52, 0.72)` with backdrop blur
- **Surface Level 3 (Elevated Tooltips / Chips / Flyouts):** `rgba(35, 46, 70, 0.85)`
- **Border / Stroke Light:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` for passive separation; `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)` on hover/focus.
- **AI Glowing Border:** Linear gradient `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.4)` to `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4)`.

### Typography Colors
- **Text High-Contrast:** `#FFFFFF` (pure white, primary titles and scores)
- **Text Medium-Contrast:** `#94A3B8` (slate, secondary labels, metadata, specs)
- **Text Low-Contrast:** `#64748B` (muted metadata, inactive states, fine print)

## Typography

The typographical engine pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural brand identity, headers, and quick-scan data points with **Inter** for dense culinary descriptions and conversational AI reasoning.

### Typography Applications
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Selected for its geometric stance and contemporary warmth. It delivers punchy restaurant names, dish highlights, numerical ratings, and badges without feeling industrial.
- **Inter:** Provides maximum legibility at small sizes across dark canvases. Used for taste notes, review excerpts, sommelier pairing suggestions, and generative reasoning prose.
- **Tabular Figures:** All numeric rating readouts, pricing tags, and distance metrics must enforce tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent layout shifts during live filtering.
- **Micro-Caps:** Use `label-sm` with uppercase transformation and `0.06em` letter spacing for categorization flags (e.g., "AI MATCH CONFIDENCE", "CHEF'S TABLE", "MICHELIN GUIDE").

## Layout & Spacing

The platform follows a responsive 12-column fluid grid system on desktop that gracefully collapses to an 8-column system on tablet and a 4-column system on mobile devices.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile` (1rem / 16px), `gutter-sm` (1rem / 16px). Single-column stack for restaurant lists and AI reasoning panels.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `margin-tablet` (1.5rem / 24px), `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px). Split-screen discovery with scrollable preview cards and collapsible AI prompts.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `margin` (2rem / 32px), `gutter-lg` (2rem / 32px). Max content width constrained to `1440px` centered. 4 columns allocated to persistent AI filters & reasoning, 8 columns reserved for restaurant exploration feeds and sensory data visualizations.

### Spatial Rhythm Rules
- Component internal padding uses strictly `space-sm` (8px) for compact tags, `space-md` (16px) for standard cards, and `space-lg` (24px) for hero and AI highlighted containers.
- Content section stacks strictly use `space-xl` (40px) to guarantee optical breathing room amidst dark, high-contrast imagery.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy on dark interfaces is defined by light permeability, chromatic saturation, and specular boundary definitions rather than muddy drop shadows.

### Glassmorphic Layers & Backdrop Blur
- **Ground Floor (Base Canvas):** Solid `#0B0F17`. Augmented with static or subtle animated radial gradients: `radial-gradient(circle at 10% 20%, rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.08) 0%, transparent 40%)` and `radial-gradient(circle at 90% 80%, rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.06) 0%, transparent 40%)`.
- **Card Tier 1 (Passive Glass):** Background `rgba(26, 34, 52, 0.55)`, backdrop blur `16px`, subtle border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`. No drop shadow.
- **Card Tier 2 (Interactive / Hover):** Background `rgba(26, 34, 52, 0.85)`, backdrop blur `24px`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **AI Special Elevation (DineSense Active Cards):** Background `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(26, 34, 52, 0.8) 0%, rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.9) 100%)`, backdrop blur `24px`. Border: `1px solid transparent` wrapped in an inner stroke illusion using pseudo-elements with `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.5), rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.3))`. Outer glow: `0 0 24px -2px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.18)`.
- **Floating Modals & Overlays:** Background `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.95)`, backdrop blur `32px`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`. Shadow: `0 24px 64px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined modern curvature (`roundedness: 2`), balancing organic culinary indulgence with algorithmic structure.

### Radius System
- **Base Components (`rounded` - 0.5rem / 8px):** Form controls, text input boxes, dropdown options, and micro badges.
- **Cards & Surfaces (`rounded-lg` - 1rem / 16px):** Standard restaurant cards, AI recommendation capsules, menus, review modules, and modal containers.
- **Hero & Feature Containers (`rounded-xl` - 1.5rem / 24px):** Major showcase spotlights, interactive taste wheels, and booking flow dialogs.
- **Pills (`rounded-full`):** Filter tags, conversational prompt starters, live availability badges, and interactive navigation bars.
